Parents' Corner 🌿

KindQuest teaches children to ask before they touch — and to read when an animal says "not right now." The best next step is meeting a real rescued animal and practising it for real.

Before you go

  • Let the animal come to you — never chase or corner them.
  • Ask a caretaker before touching or feeding anyone.
  • Watch the ears and the head: turning away means "give me space."
  • Quiet voices. Sudden noises are frightening to prey animals.
  • "No" from an animal is not rejection — it's communication. 💛
